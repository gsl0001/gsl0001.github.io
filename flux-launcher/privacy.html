Flux

Privacy policy

Flux 0.2.0 · Last updated 7 October 2026

In short

Flux has no account, no telemetry and no analytics. It does not collect personal data, and nothing it stores leaves your PC.

What Flux stores on your PC

Everything below lives in your user profile and is never uploaded. Settings › Privacy in the app lists each store with its path and size.

  • Your settings, pinned apps, workspaces and snippets.
  • How often you open each result, so the ones you use most rank first.
  • A cache of app icons.
  • Log files for troubleshooting. Search queries are never written to them.
  • Clipboard history, only if you turn it on. It is off until you answer the question during setup.

What Flux sends over the network

A shipping build makes three kinds of request, and no others.

RequestWhenGoes to
Checks for a new version of FluxOnly while update checks are on. You can turn them off.github.com
Opens a web search in your browserOnly when you press Enter on a "search the web" resultwww.google.com, through your browser
Looks up your place and its weatherOnly while the weather card is on and you have set a placeOpen-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com, geocoding-api.open-meteo.com)

Those services receive what any web request carries, such as your IP address, and the place name for the weather lookup. Their own privacy policies apply to it. Flux sends them nothing else.

Children

Flux does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
